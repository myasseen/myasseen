## About

I'm a Senior Product Designer based in the UAE, working across product strategy, UX/UI, complex workflows, design systems, and digital products.

My approach is practical and systems-oriented: understand the real problem, simplify what matters, create a clear structure, and build solutions that teams can use, maintain, and evolve.

I work at the intersection of **product thinking, UX/UI, systems, and technical feasibility**.

## Principles

- **Clarity over complexity** — solve the important problem first.
- **Systems over isolated screens** — design for consistency and evolution.
- **Useful over impressive** — make the product easier to understand, build, and use.
- **Iterate with evidence** — ship a strong practical solution, learn, then improve.
